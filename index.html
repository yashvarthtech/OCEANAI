Problem Context ·

The Critical Challenges Facing Life Below Water

Marine ecosystems face a compounding triple crisis of illegal extraction, chemical pollution, and artisanal economic marginalization.

90%
Predatory Fish Depleted Globally
Decades of unregulated bottom-trawling have destroyed marine breeding grounds. Rogue industrial trawlers routinely breach shallow artisanal sanctuaries at night without detection.
  • Nocturnal AIS Dark Fleets: Vessels switch off transponders inside marine protected zones.
  • Bycatch Devastation: Juvenile fish and endangered sea turtles trapped in non-selective nets.
11M+
Tons of Plastic & Chemical Runoff Yearly
Industrial effluent, untreated municipal waste, and ballast oil discharge cause acidification, severe hypoxia (dead zones), and devastating coral reef bleaching.
  • Delayed Detection: Ocean pollution events take days or weeks to be noticed manually.
  • Mangrove Dieback: Coastal buffer trees poisoned before root filtration can adapt.
1 in 5
Fish Caught Illegally or Unreported
Seafood supply chains remain an opaque black box. Consumers cannot verify if their purchase was caught lawfully or through predatory habitat destruction.
3.3 Billion
People Rely on Marine Protein for Nutrition
Small-scale fishers are the frontline guardians of the ocean, yet government regulations treat them as violators instead of empowering them as conservation partners.
